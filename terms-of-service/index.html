Service Agreements & Policies

1. Scope of Services

DE electrics provides comprehensive electrical services including wiring, lighting installation, power systems, switchboard upgrades, and general maintenance for residential and commercial properties in Berlin. All work is performed by qualified professionals in accordance with applicable German electrical standards.

2. Safety and Compliance

Safety is our absolute priority. DE electrics operates under strict Work Health and Safety (WHS) protocols. Clients are required to provide safe access to the site and disclose any known hazards (e.g., asbestos, faulty existing wiring) before work commences. We reserve the right to suspend services if site conditions are deemed unsafe.

3. Liability and Warranty

We stand by the quality of our workmanship. Any warranty period will be stated in the applicable quote or agreement. DE electrics is not liable for issues arising from pre-existing electrical faults not addressed during the service or for damages caused by third-party interference after our technicians have completed their work.

4. Quotations and Payment

Quotes are valid for 30 days from the date of issue. Any variations to the scope of work requested during the project will be billed accordingly. Payment is due upon completion of the service unless otherwise agreed in writing. Late payments may incur administrative fees.

5. Privacy and Data

DE electrics respects your privacy. Any personal information collected during the booking process is used solely for service delivery and billing purposes and will not be shared with third parties without your explicit consent, except as required by applicable German or European law.

Legal Inquiries

For any questions regarding these terms or our services, please contact DE electrics through our contact page.
