Homeowner Safety Guidelines

Protecting your home starts with awareness. DE electrics follows a step-by-step best practice protocol during every installation and repair to eliminate risks before they arise. We provide homeowners with practical safety guidelines to maintain a secure electrical environment.

  • Never overload power boards or double adapters.
  • Test your safety switches every three months.
  • Identify flickering lights as early warning signs.
